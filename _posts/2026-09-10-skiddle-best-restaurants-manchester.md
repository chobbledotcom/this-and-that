**Where:** **3 Soap St, Manchester M4 1EW**

"This & That opened in Manchester in 1984 and has not only stood the test of time as a cornerstone of the city's food scene, but has also been hailed as the best curry spot in town by generations of Mancs. Famous for their rice and three, This & That was one of, if not the, first to introduce this relaxed, home-cooking style of South Asian food to Manchester. Their buttery naan, rich rotating curry menu, refreshingly sweet mango lassi, and even sweeter prices will leave you wanting for nothing. If you try one spot on this list, let it be this family-run staple."
