**Visiting Us**

- **What is a "rice and three"?**  
Rice with any three curries from that day's choice - from £6.00. See [today's curry menu](/menu/)
- **Where are you?**  
3 Soap St, Manchester M4 1EW - a back street in the [Northern Quarter](/), next to Trof and 30 seconds from Shudehill Metrolink
- **What time are you open?**  
11:30am to 8:00pm Sunday to Thursday, and 11:30am to 9:00pm on Friday and Saturday
- **How long have you been open?**  
We're a family run business, established in **1984**

**Bookings / Reservations**

- **Do I need to book?**  
We do not take bookings
- **Can I make a reservation?**  
We do not take reservations
- **Do you have space for a large group?**  
Probably! It can get busy at lunch
- **Can you cater our event?**  
Probably! Please visit the cafe to arrange

**Dietary / Allergies**

- **Is your meat Halal?**  
All of our meat is [HMC Halal Certified](https://halalhmc.org/) - read more about our [halal curry](/halal-curry/)
- **Are your curries vegan?**  
Our vegetable curries are all [vegan](/vegan-curry/), and we cook three of them every day
- **Do your curries contain nuts?**  
None of our curries contain nuts
- **Do the curries contain gluten?**  
None of the curries contain gluten (breads and sundies do) - see our [gluten free curries](/gluten-free-curries/)
- **I have a very severe allergy, can you accommodate?**  
We cannot guarantee no cross-contamination
